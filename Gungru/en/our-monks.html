A Community of Over 30 Monks

The monks of Gungru Khangtsen come from across the Tibetan plateau and the Himalayan regions. They undergo decades of intensive study in Buddhist philosophy, logic, and debate at one of the great monastic universities founded by Je Tsongkhapa in 1409. Their dedication preserves an unbroken lineage of wisdom for future generations.
