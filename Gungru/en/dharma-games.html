Created with the Monks

Every game in the Dharma Games collection is created in partnership with the monks of Gaden Shartse Gungru Khangtsen Monastery. The teachings, stories, and wisdom woven into each experience are authentic reflections of the Gelug tradition of Tibetan Buddhism. These games are offered freely as a way to share the Dharma with the world.
