Preserving a Living Tradition

Gungru Khangtsen is one of eleven monastic houses within Gaden Shartse Monastery in Mundgod, India — home to the exiled Tibetan monastic community since 1966. Our monks dedicate their lives to the study of Buddhist philosophy, debate, and meditation, preserving a tradition stretching back over six centuries.
