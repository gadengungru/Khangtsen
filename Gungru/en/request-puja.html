🙏 Sacred Ceremonies

What is a Puja?

A Puja is a traditional Tibetan Buddhist prayer ceremony performed by monks to generate positive energy, accumulate merit, and invoke the blessings of enlightened beings. Pujas can be dedicated for healing, protection from obstacles, spiritual progress, or the benefit of loved ones — both living and deceased.

When you request a Puja, our monks gather in the prayer hall to chant sacred texts, make offerings, and perform rituals that have been passed down through an unbroken lineage for centuries. The collective prayers of our monastic community carry profound spiritual power and blessing.

☸ Available Ceremonies

Puja Types

Choose from our traditional prayer ceremonies, each with its own unique benefits and blessings.

🏥

Medicine Buddha Puja

For healing and good health. The Medicine Buddha’s blessings are invoked to aid recovery from illness, promote physical and mental well-being, and remove the suffering of disease.

🌿

Tara Puja

For protection and removing obstacles. Green Tara, the Swift Liberator, is called upon to shield from fears, dangers, and hindrances on one’s spiritual and worldly path.

📖

Manjushri Puja

For wisdom and academic success. Manjushri, the Bodhisattva of Wisdom, is invoked to sharpen intelligence, improve memory, and cultivate deep understanding of the Dharma.

💙

Chenrezig Puja

For compassion and harmony. Avalokiteshvara, the Buddha of Compassion, is invoked to cultivate loving-kindness, heal relationships, and bring peace to families and communities.

🙏

Lama Choepa (Guru Puja)

For spiritual progress and merit. This profound practice strengthens the connection with one’s spiritual teachers and accumulates vast merit for enlightenment.

🕯

Butter Lamp Offering

For dispelling darkness and illumination. The flame of a butter lamp symbolizes the light of wisdom that dispels the darkness of ignorance and guides beings toward liberation.

What Happens Next?

After you submit your request, our monastery administrator will review the details and confirm the puja arrangement via email. The monks will perform the ceremony on the requested date (or the next auspicious date if no preference is given).

If you opted to make a donation, you will be contacted with donation instructions. Donations are entirely voluntary — prayers are offered freely to all who request them.
