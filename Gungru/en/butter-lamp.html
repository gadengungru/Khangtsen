The Tradition of Butter Lamp Offerings

In Tibetan Buddhism, lighting a butter lamp is one of the most sacred and widely practiced offerings. The flame of a butter lamp symbolizes the dispelling of the darkness of ignorance, just as the light of wisdom illuminates the path to enlightenment. Each lamp offered is a prayer made visible — a luminous expression of devotion and compassion.

The act of offering light is considered to generate great merit, benefiting not only the one who offers but all sentient beings. Traditionally, butter lamps are lit before sacred images and texts as a way to honor the Buddha's teachings, dedicate prayers for loved ones, and cultivate the inner light of awareness.

When you light a lamp here, our monks at Gaden Shartse Gungru Khangtsen Monastery will light a physical butter lamp on your behalf in the prayer hall, accompanying it with prayers and dedications according to your intention.
